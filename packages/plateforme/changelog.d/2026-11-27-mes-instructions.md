### Assistants
- `context` serves a rule: « remember that… », « from now on… » or « my instructions » is written in `private/<handle>/contexte` with `write`, not in the assistant's memory, and said.

### Hosts
- UI: the private Context shows as « Mes instructions » in the rail, its title and its page, which carries a sentence to copy into the assistant to move the preferences it keeps there. `PiedDuRail` takes an optional `handle`: with it, « Mes instructions » opens the account menu.
