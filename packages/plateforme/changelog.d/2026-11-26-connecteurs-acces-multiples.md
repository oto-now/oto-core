### Hosts
- UI: on the Connectors screen, a tool that opens by several accesses has one card in « Ajouter un connecteur », never one per access; its sheet says « Deux façons de se connecter » and lets the person choose the way in radio cards (oneself first), the body of the chosen way remounted on a change. A way is said by how it connects and for whom (« Avec mon compte <outil> », « Avec ma clé », « Avec une clé », « Avec une clé, pour l'organisation », « Avec une clé, pour une équipe »), never by the access's declared label, which only tells apart two ways that would read the same. In the list, an account of such a tool says its way after the tool (« · clé », « · connexion au service »). An account that retains no access of a tool that now opens by several says « À remplacer », first in the list, without a gesture. The connection page says each access of `choose_access` by the same sentence.
- Schemas: `ConnectAccessChoice` gains `owner` (optional: the kind of the space the access's plan retains), served by `choose_access`.
- UI: in the « Ajouter un connecteur » sheet, the share decides the owner at creation, with no owner menu any more: opened to the whole organisation by an administrator, the connector is the organisation's; shared to a single team the person may endow, that team's; otherwise it stays theirs, shared as chosen (several teams included; always theirs at a third party). « Ouvrir à toute l'organisation » replaces the « Aucun accès » line; the « Ajouter une personne ou une équipe » field also offers « Toute l'organisation », and what the person may not give (the organisation for a non-administrator, a team they do not endow) is offered greyed, with its reason, by keyboard as by pointer. The name is always asked, proposed after that owner (« <outil> de <prénom> », « <outil> · <équipe> », « <outil> · <organisation> », numbered in brackets after that owner's connectors of the tool, « Pennylane · Démo (3) ») and follows the share until the person types one. In a connector's drawer: a pencil for « Modifier le nom », « Remplacer la clé » next to « Tester », the share field and list directly under « Qui peut s'en servir » (no « Partager » button nor dialog), « Journal des partages » as a link. The level menu shows all its choices, without a scrollbar.
- UI: `DonneesDesConnecteurs` gains `organisation` (optional): the organisation's name, for the proposed name of an organisation connector; absent, « <outil> de l'organisation ». A host passes `organisation: identity.org.name` (the reference host does).
- UI: `ChampDAjoutDAcces` gains two optional entries, `organisation` (« Toute l'organisation » among the answers) and a `refus` per candidate (greyed, `aria-disabled`, its reason described); a page's share passes neither and is unchanged.
- UI: on the Connectors screen, the whole row of a connector opens its drawer (the name's link stays the only keyboard target; the row's gestures keep their action); `table.css` gains the opt-in `data-ligne-ouvrante` (pointer on the row).
