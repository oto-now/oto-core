### Hosts
- UI: the screen of a content (`EcranDeNoeud`) is flat: no right column, no card around the text; the title once, without glyph, the meta under it (« Procédure · Modifiée il y a 3 jours par Claire · Ventes »), then the text and a foot « Sous-pages · N », « Cité dans N contenus », « Cite N contenus ».
- UI: the header of a content holds Commentaires, Liens (with a popover), Historique des versions, « Partager » (filled, label only) and « ⋯ »; download, « Transformer en procédure / en page », « Importer un fichier… » and « Réglages du tableau… » (a dialog) moved into « ⋯ ».
- UI: comments open in a panel beside the text from 1 024 px (a drawer below), on arrival only when a thread is unread for the person; hovering a block shows « Commenter ce bloc » and « Historique du bloc ».
- UI: `EcranDeNoeud` takes `fils`, the promise of `listComments` for the node, started by the host page before it counts the view (`recordNodeView` in `after`), not awaited: without it, the panel reads the threads once hydrated, after the view counted, and no thread is unread.
- Server: `listComments` is exported; each `CommentThreadView` carries `unread` (open, meant for the caller, and a comment of another after their last visit of the page: the rule of the home's « Commentaires »).
- UI: `AnnexesDuContexte` takes `place?: "tete" | "pied"`: pass it twice, in `annexes` (default, under the meta) and with `place="pied"` in `piedDeColonne` (« Ce que reçoit votre assistant »: the gauge, then the reading order); a host that keeps `JaugeDuBudget` in `piedDeColonne` shows the gauge without the reading order.
- UI: the design system gains `ui/ds/components/css/node-page.css` (flat page, header gestures, foot, comments panel); `OptionsDuTableau` is replaced by `ReglagesDuTableau` (`open`, `onClose`), internal to the package.
