### Hosts
- UI: under `.oto`, Tailwind's text sizes (`text-xs` to `text-4xl`) and radii (`rounded-sm` to `rounded-xl`) now follow the design system's scales (`--fs-*`, `--r-container`) instead of Tailwind's and the host's `--radius`; outside `.oto`, nothing changes.
- UI: the design system gains a text link (`.oto-link`, underlined at rest, in the colour of its text, behind `LIEN` and `LienDeCellule`) and an island intertitle role (`.oto-section-title`); `.oto-label` is now in `--mute`, at 4.5:1 at least in every theme.
